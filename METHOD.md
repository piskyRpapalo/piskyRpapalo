# Build with us — the PreceptorOS method

This is not a code of conduct for behavior. It is a method for building
things that stay true after you walk away — implemented, not imagined.

**1 · Propose in typed contracts.** Close the input and close the
output. Anything the model cannot ground fails closed to `NO DATA` —
with the cause, never a guess. *(Robustness — EU AI Act art. 15)*

**2 · Local first.** What we build runs on the machine its owner
controls. Zero telemetry, zero external requests on load. *(Data
protection by design — GDPR art. 25; nothing leaves the node — ISO/IEC
27001)*

**3 · Two passes.** Run the full suite twice before claiming anything.
*(Validation before deployment — ISO/IEC 42001)*

**4 · Red before green.** Write the test that fails first. A suite that
cannot go red cannot protect anything. *(Risk management in the small)*

**5 · The PR carries its own measurement.** It states what passed, what
was skipped and why, and what it deliberately did not do. *(Record-
keeping and technical documentation — EU AI Act art. 12)*

**6 · Silicon proposes, carbon signs.** Every consequential change waits
for a human signature. No autonomous merges, no self-approved value, no
machine that acts because it believed it had permission. Believing is
not having. *(Human oversight — EU AI Act art. 14)*

**7 · Name the state.** Everything you publish carries its state —
measured, declared, proposed, simulated, stale, or no-data-with-cause.
*(Transparency — EU AI Act art. 50)*

One naming rule travels with the method: **titles teach what a piece
does** — `human_oversight_gate`, `ledger_append_only` — in plain
descriptive English. And one golden rule closes it: we claim a design
posture, not compliance. Compliance is counsel, and it stays `NO DATA`
until a human lawyer signs it.
