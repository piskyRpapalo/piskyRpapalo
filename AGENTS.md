## The discipline of the house (amendment · 2026-10-28)

1. ODD is law: explore, implement, check — every piece of work. One feature per feature document, one work unit per commit, one slice per PR. The signature gates the MERGE, never the BUILDING: work never halts for a signature; register the question, take the lawful default, continue.
2. Silicon never signs. The Sovereign signs pieces of value, per piece and per exact head; batches expire. The Desk serves fence lines and merges what is signed. Docs-only PRs merge under the standing ODD FIRMO, cited in the receipt.
3. Honest measurement: NO_DATA with cause is a lawful state. What is reported was measured; a gate that never ran did not pass; a fixture shown as a model's answer is a lie the house will not serve.
4. SCRUB every public artifact: English or the house's own names; no device names; no private paths; the vetted words never appear (the canonical list lives in the gate fixtures of the repos that gate).
5. Receipts are the memory of the house: short, measured, under 1,024 bytes, with cause. The public doctrine lives in the study; the Desk's status board (the two readers' harness) lives in odd/STATUS.md of the web repository.
